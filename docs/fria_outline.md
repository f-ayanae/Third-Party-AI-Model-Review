# Fundamental rights impact assessment: outline (AI Act Art 27)

Status: outline only. Needs evidence requests E04 and E05 to complete. Owner: Head of Compliance (AI governance). Notify BaFin before first use, if the score is in scope.

| Art 27(1) element | Content for the Ratenkredit |
|---|---|
| (a) Process | Origination: auto-decline below the lower cut-off, pricing band, maximum loan amount. The middle band (about 20%) goes to an underwriter. |
| (b) Period and frequency | Continuous from go-live; about 120,000 applications a year. Reviewed annually and after any model version change. |
| (c) Categories of persons affected | German-resident applicants aged 18 and over. Named groups: under-25s, residents under 3 years, the self-employed, and people without a credit card or bank contract. |
| (d) Specific risks of harm | Exclusion or higher pricing for short-history applicants without a matching risk difference (F03). The address criterion is under legal question (F01). Data errors that are slow to correct (F11). The applicant's own loan term lowers future scores (F13). Possible age-proxy effects from time-based criteria. |
| (e) Human oversight | Refer band with override authority; underwriter training on the 12 criteria; §37a(5) BDSG and §505a BGB human-review route; override monitoring. |
| (f) Measures if risks materialise | Contest and correction route with a bureau SLA; complaint analysis by segment; PSI and approval-rate thresholds with named owners; suspension trigger under Art 26(5); notice to the bureau and BaFin. |

Evidence still needed: segment default rates and score distributions (E04), the bureau's bias examination (E05), instructions for use (E12).
