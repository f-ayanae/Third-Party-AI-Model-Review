# Role map

| Party | AI Act | GDPR and German law | DORA, MaRisk and other |
|---|---|---|---|
| The bureau | Provider (if the score is an AI system; precautionary position: yes). EU-based. | Independent controller (credit agency). Creator of the score under §37a BDSG from 20 Nov 2026, so owes the §37a(4) explanation on request. Under C-634/21, its score can be an Art 22 decision where a lender relies on it decisively. | ICT third-party service provider (assessment). Supports a critical or important function. AT 9 does not apply (MaRisk 06/2026). |
| Mainkai Direktbank AG (fictional) | Deployer: Arts 4, 26, 27 (FRIA, notified to BaFin), 86. | Controller for the credit decision. User of the score under §37a BDSG: input limits, s.37a(5) contest and human decision. §505a BGB creditworthiness test. Its own Art 22 decision for auto-declines. | Financial entity under DORA: Art 28 register, Art 30 contract, Art 28(8) exit. Model owner under MaRisk and EBA GL/2020/06. |
| Applicants | Affected persons (Art 86) | Data subjects (Arts 15(1)(h), 22). §37a(4) and (5) rights. | Consumers under §§491 ff. BGB |
| BaFin | Market surveillance authority (KI-MIG, from 29 Jul 2026) | Not applicable (data protection authorities are separate) | Prudential and conduct supervisor |

**Role trap.** If Mainkai blended the bureau's score with its own data into a new scoring model, Mainkai would become the provider of that combined system. Scope decision: the score is used as delivered, and cut-offs are policy rules, not a model change.

**Classification chain.** Is it an AI system under Art 3(1)? Contested; precautionary yes. Annex III 5(b)? Yes. Art 6(3)? Unavailable, because the score profiles natural persons. When? From 2 Dec 2027, and for this legacy system only after a significant design change (Art 111(2)).
