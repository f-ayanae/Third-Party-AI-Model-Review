## Facts extracted

- AT 2.1 Tz. 1: applies to institutions that are not significant institutions under the SSM Regulation.
- AT 4.3.4 (use of models) Tz. 1: covers models used in the circular's processes, including automated models and AI; a model includes one developed by a third party, for example for credit granting; CRR models are excluded.
- AT 4.3.4 Tz. 2: suitability is assessed before use and reviewed regularly, which requires sufficient knowledge of the model's design, key assumptions, parameters and input data.
- AT 4.3.4 Tz. 3 to 6: data-quality procedures; rules for using model results including overrides; regular validation of accuracy, stability and consistency, including after recalibration; sufficient explainability, meaning input-output relationships can be shown.
- AT 9 Tz. 1: outsourcing means another firm performs activities the institution would otherwise perform itself; other external procurement (sonstiger Fremdbezug) is not outsourcing.
- AT 9 Tz. 1 note: outsourced or procured ICT services under Art 3(21) DORA subject to Arts 28-30 DORA are outside AT 9.
- BTO 1.1 Tz. 6: decision powers and any use of automated models must be clearly described.
- BTO 1.2 Tz. 1: credit-risk procedures must set conditions for automated decision procedures (products, segments, limits) and governance for automated models and their model risk.
- BTO 1.2 Tz. 5: the institution must form its own judgement of default risk and may not rely exclusively on external credit assessments.
