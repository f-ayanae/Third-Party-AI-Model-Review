# L24: Research note: s.30(7) BDSG as amended by BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 139 (Art 3 No. 2)

## Facts extracted

- s.30(7) BDSG replaces the former s.30(2) and implements the CCD2 rule on rejections based on database consultation.
- Trigger: a lender rejects a consumer credit application as a result of information from a credit agency or database operator (extended to paid financing assistance by s.30(9)).
- The lender must inform the consumer without undue delay of the rejection and the information obtained, details of the database consulted, and the categories of personal data taken into account.
- No duty where disclosure would endanger public security or order (carried over from the former s.30(2) sentence 2).
- Act page on recht.bund.de: promulgated 12 May 2026, published 18 May 2026.
- Correction after reading the act (L01): the promulgated s.30(7) contains no public-security carve-out, and it has a second sentence on rejections resting on automated processing that this note omits.
