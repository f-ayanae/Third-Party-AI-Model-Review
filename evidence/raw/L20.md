## Facts extracted

- Describes the consultation draft of the 9th MaRisk revision of 1 April 2026; consultation ran to 8 May 2026.
- MaRisk would no longer apply to significant institutions.
- Size classes: very small institutions (four-year average balance sheet up to EUR 1bn) and small institutions (SNCIs under Art 4(1) No. 145 CRR); other less significant institutions get no specific facilitations.
- The former AT 4.3.4 on data management, data quality and risk data aggregation would be deleted, as it was aimed at significant institutions.
- An outsourcing officer would no longer be required under MaRisk; the outsourcing register remains required under s.25b(1) sentence 4 KWG.
