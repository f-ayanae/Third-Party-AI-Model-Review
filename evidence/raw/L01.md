## Facts extracted

- Art 3 No. 2 replaces s.30(2) BDSG with new paragraphs (2) to (9); No. 3 deletes s.31; No. 4 inserts s.37a (Scoring).
- s.37a(2) No. 1: probability values may be created or used only if the following are not used: (a) Art 9(1) GDPR data, (b) age, gender, name or social network data, (c) information on payments into and out of bank accounts, (d) address data ('Anschriftendaten'); No. 2: not about minors; No. 3: data demonstrably relevant under a scientifically recognised mathematical-statistical method and not used for other purposes.
- s.37a(4): the creator of probability values must on request state the data and criteria used, the weighting of the most influential criteria, the meaning of the value, and the values created and their recipients, within the Art 12(3) GDPR deadline; the information is kept for one year.
- s.37a(5): against a controller, the person has the right to contest a decision based on probability values, to state a view and to a decision by a natural person.
- s.30(5): lenders and credit intermediaries may not process Art 9(1) GDPR data or social network data that may be held in credit-agency databases.
- s.30(6): where the creditworthiness assessment for a general consumer loan involves automated processing, the borrower may demand human intervention, including a clear explanation of the assessment and the logic and risks of the automated processing, the right to state a view and a review; the lender must inform the borrower of these rights after the automated processing.
- s.30(7) sentence 1: whoever rejects a consumer loan as a result of credit-agency information must inform the consumer without delay of the rejection, the information obtained, details of the database consulted and the data categories considered.
- s.30(7) sentence 2: where the creditworthiness assessment otherwise rests on automated processing, the lender must inform the borrower of a rejection and, for general consumer loans, of the right to review by a person and the procedure to contest the decision.
- s.43(1) No. 2 BDSG (as amended): failing to inform under s.30(3) sentence 2 No. 1, s.30(6) sentence 3 or s.30(7) is an administrative offence.
- Art 4: s.2(2) No. 14 UKlaG now covers ss.30 and 37a BDSG (actions by consumer associations).
- Art 16(1): the act enters into force on 20 November 2026, with listed exceptions on the day after promulgation.
