## Facts extracted

- Score classes: 776 to 999 excellent (about 62%); 709 to 775 good (about 20%); 642 to 708 acceptable (about 8%); 100 to 641 sufficient (about 2%).
- No score is calculated where payment defaults are open; about 8% of people in Germany currently have open payment defaults.
- The score is built from twelve criteria; the page does not list their point values.
- Companies have a transition period to the end of 2028; consumers and companies see the same score.
- An explanation tool in the consumer account discloses the formula.
